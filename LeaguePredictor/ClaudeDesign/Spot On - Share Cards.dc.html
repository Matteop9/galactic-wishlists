Wiring notes
  • Fonts in next/og must be fetched as ArrayBuffers — Archivo 900 + Plex Sans 400/600, subset to Latin
  • Titles are 88px (OG) and 118px (invite); step down one size past 18 characters so three-word league names still fit two lines
  • Max 5 avatars then +n in primary; initials only, never photos (Satori won’t crop them reliably)
  • WhatsApp re-encodes to ~400px wide — nothing below 25px type survives it
  • Set og:image:width/height explicitly or iMessage falls back to a small card
