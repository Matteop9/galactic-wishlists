Logo & wordmark

The mark is a position marker with the dot dead centre — zero deviation, spot on. Offset the dot and you have the whole scoring system in one shape: it is the same geometry that drives the diff chips.

Mark · lockup rules
  • Clear space = ring diameter ÷ 2 on all sides
  • Wordmark: Archivo 800, uppercase, ‑0.04em tracking
  • Hairspace between “Spot” and “On”, never a full space
  • Never outline, rotate or gradient the mark
