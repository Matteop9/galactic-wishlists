# 2026/27 Accumulator Challenge — Official Rules

**Season 7 & 8 · VDL v JHP**

Each participant contributes one leg to their team's six-fold acca each gameweek. Two teams of six; individual and team leaderboards run all season. £2.50 minimum stake. By submitting a pick, you agree to these rules.

## 1. Selection deadlines

- The selection window opens **Thursday 6:00 PM UK** and closes **Friday 8:00 PM UK**.
- Picks are made **in the group chat** by that deadline; entering them into the app stays open until **Saturday midnight UK** (they'll already have been picked in the chat).
- Only matches kicking off **Saturday 2:00 PM UK or later** are eligible.
- Internationals are excluded, except during international breaks (see Section 11).
- Selections must be eligible for the **Bet365 Winnings Boost**, except during international breaks.

## 2. Odds criteria

- Minimum odds **1.50** (decimal). No maximum.
- If you state odds, they're locked in. No odds stated = pick not confirmed (treated as no pick).
- Picks may be challenged for **one hour** after submission; egregious issues can go to a third party at any time regardless.
- If odds have moved, you get the **lower** odds unless you have a screenshot timestamped within an hour before your message.

## 3. Forfeits

- A seasonal forfeit is agreed by the group before Gameweek 1; all must commit to it.
- Each team can request proof of the 12-fold bet via screen recording. Failure to provide = **−1 point**.
- **Mid-season forfeit — Saturday 16 January 2027, London** *(date to be confirmed by group)*: losing team buys bowling + dinner, £100 budget for the day.
- **End-of-season playoffs**: the bottom 3 individuals on the all-season combined table play off with blind picks submitted to a mediator; the loser performs an individual forfeit.

## 4. Points & scoring

- Your score for a winning pick is its **decimal odds**; a losing pick scores **0**.
- Individual leaderboard rank = total score. **Score per Match** (total score ÷ entries) is published alongside as the fairness metric where entry counts differ.
- Team score = sum of all six members' scores.
- Bonuses and penalties (Section 3, Section 6) are applied via the adjustments log with a stated reason. No penalty has been required since the rules were formalised in Season 5 — long may it continue.

- **6/6 double:** if all six of a team's picks win in a gameweek, every member's winning odds for that week are **doubled**. (Applied three times to date, all by VDL: 13 Dec 2025, 7 Feb 2026, 28 Feb 2026.) The final gameweek of each season is exempt from doubling.

## 5. Tie-breaker

Ties at season end are settled by a tie-breaker round the following week: picks are made from a designated set of matches, and the winner is whoever's pick wins.

## 6. Invalid picks

- Any pick not meeting Section 2 criteria is invalid. It may be replaced before the deadline.
- A match cancelled or postponed **after** the deadline renders the pick invalid.
- An invalid pick scores **0**.
- **No pick submitted = −2 points** *(codified from last season's practice; the old written rule said −3 but −2 is what was applied — confirm with group if anyone objects)*.

## 7. Substitute picks

If a match is cancelled or postponed **before** the Friday 8:00 PM deadline, a substitute pick may be submitted before the deadline.

## 8. Gameweeks

- Gameweeks follow Premier League weekends.
- Weeks can be included or skipped by majority vote (**4 of 6 per team**).
- Note for 2026/27: the PL calendar has 33 weekend rounds and five midweek rounds; midweek rounds are skipped by default unless voted in.

## 9. Season duration

The 2026/27 Premier League runs 21/22 August 2026 to 30 May 2027 (one week later than usual after the World Cup).

- **Season 7 (Season 1 of 2026/27):** Saturday 22 August – Saturday 19 December 2026. Playoffs 20–26 December (not in league scoring).
- **Season 8 (Season 2 of 2026/27):** Saturday 2 January – Saturday 22 May 2027. Playoffs 23–29 May (not in league scoring).
- The PL's final round (Sunday 30 May 2027) is outside the competition, consistent with prior seasons and the Saturday-2PM rule.

## 10. Method eligibility

Permitted pick methods, as established practice: **Win** (match result) and **BTTS** (both teams to score, both teams named). Other markets require group approval before the deadline.

## 11. International breaks

- Accas become **multisport** (any sport allowed).
- The Winnings Boost requirement (Section 1) does not apply.
- Confirmed 2026/27 international break windows to be circulated once FIFA dates are locked; the September, October, November and March windows are expected as usual.
