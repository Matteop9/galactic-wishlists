The thesis

A genuine logbook of the sky

Where the incumbent gives you a simulated catch from the sofa, SkyDex gives you a record you earned, one real sighting at a time. The brand is built on that single idea: a sighting is proven, and the proof is a stamp. Everything warm, analogue, and earned — never a slick game UI.

03 · Buttons

Controls

Two actions sit at the core of the loop. Capture is the hero — a verified real photo. Log is the casual, low-value catch from the map. The vermilion confirmed state is the success moment, and it borrows the stamp.
