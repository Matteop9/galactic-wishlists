Page anatomy — every new page

Panels are only ever a table, a list of hairline-separated rows, or a short form. Nothing else. Full rules in REDESIGN.md §6.
Not this
×Rounded pills, or any radius above 3px
×Blurred drop shadows and gradients used as decoration
×A second accent colour, or colour used for anything but status
×Sentences in the display face; uppercase sentences; type under 10px
×Emoji as ornament — flags and one empty-state glyph only
×Anything needing a build step, a dependency or a network connection
